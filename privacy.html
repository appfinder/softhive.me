Privacy Policy

SoftHive is built on a simple principle: your computer is yours. We don't track you, we don't sell data, and we don't collect anything we don't need.

Last updated: May 2025  ·  Effective immediately

info 1. Who we are

SoftHive ("we", "our", or "us") is an open-source Windows application installer developed and maintained by the SoftHive Foundation. The app is available for free at softhive.me and through GitHub.

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at contact@softhive.me.

database 2. Information we collect

The SoftHive desktop application does not collect or transmit any personal information. It runs entirely on your local machine.

The only network requests the app makes are to:

  • Fetch the public app catalog (a static list of available software and their official download URLs)
  • Download the installer for apps you explicitly select, directly from those apps' official sources
  • Check for a new version of SoftHive itself (no identifiers are sent — it's a simple version number check)

No usage data, crash reports, installed app lists, or identifiers are sent to SoftHive servers.

public 3. Website analytics

When you visit softhive.me, we may collect basic, anonymized web analytics such as page views, referring URLs, and general geographic region (country-level). This is used solely to understand how many people discover and use SoftHive.

We do not use Google Analytics or any advertising-based analytics service. If analytics are active, they are privacy-preserving (e.g. cookieless, aggregated only) and cannot be used to identify you personally.

cookie 4. Cookies

The SoftHive website does not use tracking cookies or third-party cookies. Any cookies set are strictly functional (e.g. remembering a dark-mode preference) and are never used to identify you across sessions or other sites.

share 5. Third-party software downloads

When you use SoftHive to install an app, the installer is downloaded directly from that software's official vendor (e.g. Mozilla for Firefox, Microsoft for VS Code). Those vendors operate under their own privacy policies, and SoftHive has no control over their data practices.

We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any software you install.

groups 6. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any user data with third parties. Period.

In the event we are ever required to disclose information by law (e.g. a valid court order), we will comply with applicable legal requirements. Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing meaningful to disclose.

child_care 7. Children's privacy

SoftHive is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information to us, please contact us at contact@softhive.me and we will promptly address it.

update 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We will not reduce your privacy protections without clear notice. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
