Is SoftHive free to use?

Yes. SoftHive is completely free, with no account, subscription, or hidden fees.

Where do the apps come from?

Every app is fetched directly from its official source at install time. SoftHive doesn't host modified installers or redirect downloads through mirrors.

Does SoftHive install extra toolbars or bundled software?

No. SoftHive never adds toolbars, browser extensions, or bundled offers to the apps you install.

Can I back up and restore my app list?

Yes. Save your selection as a list, then reinstall the same set of apps on a new or freshly formatted PC in a couple of clicks.

What is SoftHive built with?

SoftHive is a native desktop app built with Tauri and Rust, which keeps it lightweight, fast to start, and easy on system resources.
