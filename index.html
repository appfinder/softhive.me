Frequently asked questions

Is SoftHive free?

Yes. SoftHive is completely free to download and use, and it does not require an account.

Where do the apps come from?

Every app is downloaded directly from its official website, with no mirrors. Files are checked for authenticity and integrity before installation.

Does SoftHive install bundled software or ads?

No. SoftHive has no ads, no adware and no hidden installers or unwanted extras.

How do I reinstall my apps after formatting my PC?

Save your app list in SoftHive once. After a fresh Windows install, restore the list and reinstall everything in a few clicks.

Which versions of Windows does SoftHive support?

SoftHive works on Windows 10 and Windows 11.
